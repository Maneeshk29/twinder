How it works

Three steps, and you're always in control of who sees you.

1

Upload a selfie

A clear, front-facing photo works best. You choose whether others can match with you.

2

See your look-alikes

Twinder compares your photo with people who opted in and shows your closest matches side by side, with a similarity score.

3

Connect if you both want to

Tap "Want to meet". A chat opens only when your twin says yes too.

Try it

This prototype doesn't do real face matching yet: it shows sample matches so you can see the experience. Your photo stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

Privacy first

Look-alike matching only works if people trust it.

Opt-in only

Only people who choose to be matchable appear in anyone's results.

Mutual connections

Nobody can message you unless you both said yes.

Delete anytime

Remove your photo and profile whenever you want, and it's gone from matching.
